Same small foundation

Ten very different sites. Almost identical Nift machinery.

The visual language and runtime needs change dramatically, while the project model stays deliberately boring: tracked content, a template, small partials and ordinary web assets. Even the SPA and dashboard remain regular browser code rather than requiring a Nift-specific application framework.

project/
  .nift/
    config.json
    tracked.json
  content/
    index.html
  templates/
    template.html
    head.html
    partials/
  public/
    assets/
      css/
      js/

Readable enough to learn from. Small enough to throw away.

A starter should give you momentum without becoming an architecture you have to spend the next year escaping.
